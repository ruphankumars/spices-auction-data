Ref: 1. Public Notice No. SB/MKT/CAL/NSWS/03/2026-2029/1473 dated 23.10.26
22K
Due to certain technical issues with the National Single Window System (NSWS), applicants
had been experiencing difficulties in submitting applications for the renewal of Cardamom
Auction Licences (Manual/E-Auction) through the portal. It is, however, informed that the
technical issues have been resolved on 18.11.2026, and the portal is now fully operational for the
submission of renewal applications.
As per Rule 5(1) of the Cardamom Licensing and Marketing Rules, 1987, as amended up to
2018, any *person seeking renewal of a Cardamom Auction Licence is required to submit the
renewal application on or before the 30th day of June of the year in which the validity of the
licence expires.
In view of the delay in making the NSWS portal fully operational and the consequent difficulties
faced by applicants in submitting their renewal applications, the last date for submission of
applications for renewal of Cardamom Auction Licences (Manual/E-Auction) is hereby extended
up to 30th November 2026.
This Circular is issued for the information and necessary action of all concerned.
